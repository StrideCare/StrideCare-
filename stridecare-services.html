Our services

⭐ Medicare Bulk Billing
🩺

Physiotherapy — Medicare Bulk Billing

  • No out-of-pocket cost for eligible Medicare patients
  • Up to 5 sessions per calendar year under a GP Management Plan
  • Requires a referral from your GP (Enhanced Primary Care plan)
  • Suitable for chronic or complex conditions
  • Available in-clinic and via telehealth

Private Physiotherapy

  • No referral needed — book directly at your convenience
  • Sporting injuries, muscle strains, joint pain and rehabilitation
  • Post-surgical recovery and return-to-sport programs
  • Expert advice on movement, posture and prevention
  • Private health insurance rebates apply where applicable

NDIS
🎯

Physiotherapy — NDIS Services

  • Registered NDIS provider — physiotherapy funded under your plan
  • Goal-directed treatment aligned with your NDIS goals
  • Improve mobility, strength, function and independence
  • Work with your support coordinator and allied health team
  • In-clinic, in-home, and telehealth options available
